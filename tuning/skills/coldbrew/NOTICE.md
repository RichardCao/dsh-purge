These 86 skills are copied as a local skill pack for dsh-purge tuning.
They are not part of the upstream dsh-purge repository, and they are not
bundled into the plugin package. Import them into `$DSH_HOME/skills` with:

```bash
./tuning/scripts/import-skills.sh tuning/skills/coldbrew
```
